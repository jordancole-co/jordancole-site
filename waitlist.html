I’m looking for 8 people who know they have something they want to share with the world, that currently aren’t sharing it.

Maybe you feel stuck or scared or like you don’t have the time. Maybe you’ve tried in the past but haven’t stuck with it. Doesn’t matter. All that matters is that you’re ready to start sharing the gift you know you’re meant to be sharing.

If that's you, here is what I’m offering:

A Quest built around doing two very simple things every day for 4 weeks:

1. Embodied meditation

2. Creating a piece of content and posting it on social media

With this simple two-step approach, we aren’t going after likes or followers or recognition...

We’re looking for clarity through confrontation with what it is that actually keeps us from sharing our gift - our emotional discomfort.

By rooting ourselves in our body through meditation, then creating and posting on social media based on what we connect with, we are doing two things:

  • We are getting more comfortable being in our bodies while doing something that makes us uncomfortable.
  • We are using social media as a form of exposure therapy where we are being seen expressing ourselves authentically.

This 4-week Spirit Quest isn't about helping you get more views or gain followers, it's about building a foundation of comfort within yourself that you can use to continue to express yourself authentically for the rest of your life.

Once you start to align internally, your external experience of life will follow.

What's Included:

  • A pre-Quest intention audit so I have a feel for where you're starting from in relation to what you want to share with the world.
  • 5 weekly Zoom calls where we intentionally reconnect through embodied meditation, share our experience, and discuss what’s moving through us creatively.
  • 4 midweek Zoom group meditations with space to check-in and discuss our experience creating and posting on social media.
  • A new guided meditation audio recording each week for use in our daily practice, progressively grounding ourselves deeper in our body.
  • Access to the Enjoy the Ride WhatsApp group where we’ll be able to share our daily experience with our meditation and creation practices, holding each other accountable and supporting each other’s expansion.
