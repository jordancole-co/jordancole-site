There are a lot of flowery words I could use to try and help you feel that you are here on Earth to create the life you’ve always wanted to live.

But underneath those words, there is a very simple, grounded, two-step approach that will actually shift how you relate to your life and allow you to change how you live it.

Step 1: Consistent, embodied meditation

Step 2: Consistent, intentional creation

If you commit to 4 weeks of reconnecting to yourself through meditation, and then creating based on what you reconnect to, your life will shift. Guaranteed.

It doesn’t matter what your specific journey entails—whether you’re creating art, a business, a family, a new beginning, all of the above… life is a creative act, and treating it as one opens the doors to joy, love, peace, and all the other words that need to be felt, not read.

Reconnecting to what’s happening within us + Creating something based on what we find = Expanding our ability to enjoy the ride.

Do that consistently and the limitations you’re currently putting on what you can create with your life will drastically diminish.

What's Included:

  • A pre-quest intention audit so you and I both have a feel for where you're starting and where you'd like to go in our 4 weeks together.
  • 5 weekly Zoom calls where we intentionally reconnect to our body, each week grounding ourselves deeper through the heart, solar plexus, sacral, and root chakras.
  • 4 midweek Zoom group meditations with space to discuss our experience.
  • A new guided meditation audio recording each week to use in our daily practice.
  • Access to the Enjoy the Ride WhatsApp group where we will all be able to share our experience with our meditation and creation practices, holding each other accountable and supporting each other’s expansion.

This spirit quest is an invitation to choose to put yourself in a supportive group setting where you’re encouraged to show up by taking consistent steps back to yourself and what you’re truly capable of.

It’s simple. If you show up, your internal energy will move, and thus your external life will reflect that.

I have no idea what this process will look like for you. I don’t know what it will look like for me, either. It’s likely that discomfort and fear will show up along the way, as this tends to happen when we stretch ourselves outside of our comfort zones. I’m here to support you in this process, but I cannot change what it is that you need to confront in order to expand.

What I can tell you is that this work comes directly from my heart to yours. And I know I'm here to help remind people why we're all here… to Enjoy the Ride.
