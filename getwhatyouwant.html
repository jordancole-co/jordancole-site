The real reason you don't have what you want.

If there is something that you’ve been wanting for yourself, perhaps for a long time, but you’ve never been able to get it, do it, or achieve it... It’s NOT because there is something uniquely wrong with you.

There are over 25 years of scientific research showing it's actually because your body and mind have developed a very sophisticated system designed to keep you safe from anxiety and stress.

This system serves a vital purpose. But if you’ve never updated it, it’s still running on outdated beliefs about what is and isn't safe for you—many of which you developed when you were a child.

These outdated beliefs cause you to act in ways that no longer serve you, locking you into behavioral patterns that make it nearly impossible to get what you want out of life.

The good news is that the research proves that, even as adults, we can update this system and change our behavior.

Here’s how:

1. Choose What You Want: We start by choosing an important goal—something that, if achieved, would greatly improve our lives and the lives of the people around us.

2. Uncover the Underlying Beliefs: Once we commit to our goal, we build a “Behavioral X-Ray” by asking ourselves a series of specific questions that help us uncover the beliefs keeping us from achieving it.

3. Real-World Experiments: With more clarity on what’s keeping us stuck, we start running experiments and collecting data on what actually happens when we act in new ways that directly conflict with these outdated beliefs.

4. Consistent Integration: As we run these experiments we keep our overall goal and updated beliefs top of mind through short, daily Goal-Oriented Meditations.

This process, informed by over 25 years of developmental psychology research, is exactly what I will walk you through in this workshop.

The Bottom Line:

This work will change your life. It’s not a shortcut, and it’s not easy, though it is incredibly simple. We’re making our unconscious beliefs conscious and using that newfound awareness to reteach ourselves what we’re actually capable of.

We didn’t choose the beliefs and behaviors that run our lives, but in 2026 we all have access to the information that allows us to create new ones.
