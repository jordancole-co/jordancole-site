The real reason you don't have what you want.

The thing you think is stopping you from doing what you want to do isn't the thing that's actually stopping you. It's not a lack of discipline, laziness, or ADHD. It is a highly sophisticated system of deeply held beliefs designed to protect you from anxiety.

To actually get what you want, we have to uncover the real problem. Here is how we do it:

1. Choose the Change: Decide what you really want to change about your life. No holding back—what do you want to add or stop doing that you and the people around you believe will change your life for the better?

2. Ask the Right Questions: Uncover why your current state is making that change impossible by mapping out your hidden commitments.

3. Build the Experiment: Use those beliefs as hypotheses in your personal growth experiments. Ask yourself: If I believe this, what would happen if I acted in opposition to that belief?

4. Integrate: Anchor your goal, belief, and experiment into your daily life with short goal-oriented meditations.

During the Workshop:

  • Build a Behavioral X-Ray to uncover the hidden beliefs and assumptions that are keeping you stuck.
  • Design an Experiment to safely test the most powerful belief you uncover in your daily life.
  • Goal-Oriented Meditation to practice integrating this new subject/object distinction into your nervous system in real-time.

After the Workshop:

  • A Guided Audio Meditation to take home for your 10-minute daily integration practice.
  • The Get What You Want Blueprint for creating your own X-Rays and experiments long after the workshop is over.
