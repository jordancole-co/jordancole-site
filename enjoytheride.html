This isn’t some secret formula that, once figured out, will change everything for you. This is an invitation to choose to put yourself in a supportive group setting where you’re encouraged to show up by taking consistent steps back to yourself and what you’re truly capable of.

It’s simple. If you show up, your internal energy will move, and thus your external life will reflect that and start to shift. If you don’t show up, your energy stays in the patterns it's comfortable with, and your external life reflects that and stays the same.
